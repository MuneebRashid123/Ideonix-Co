Custom Software Development questions

Do we own the code?

Yes. The repository is yours from the first commit, along with the documentation and deployment scripts. There is no lock-in and no licence fee for the software we write for you.

What if requirements change mid-build?

They usually do. Sprints are two weeks, so changes get absorbed at the next planning session rather than derailing the schedule. Anything that materially changes scope gets re-estimated in writing before we proceed.

Can you work with our existing systems?

Almost always. If it has an API, a database or even a nightly CSV export, we can integrate it. We audit what you have during discovery and tell you honestly what is worth connecting and what is worth replacing.

Questions

Web Development questions

WordPress or a custom build?

It depends on who maintains it. If your team publishes weekly and needs full control, WordPress or a headless CMS earns its keep. If the site is mostly fixed and speed matters more, a custom Next.js build is faster and cheaper to run. We recommend based on your team, not our preference.

Will it rank on Google?

We build the foundations that let it rank — structure, speed, schema, clean markup. Ranking also depends on your content and competition, which is why we are honest that a rebuild alone rarely does it. Where clients pair the rebuild with ongoing content, results tend to follow.

Can we edit it ourselves?

Yes, that is the point. You get a CMS with the fields you need, guardrails so the layout cannot break, and a recorded walkthrough for the team.

Questions

E-Commerce Websites questions

Shopify or WooCommerce?

Shopify if you want to stop thinking about hosting and updates, and your margin absorbs the fees. WooCommerce if you need unusual logic or want to avoid per-sale costs and have someone to maintain it. We size both against your actual order volume before recommending.

How does COD verification work?

Before dispatch, the order is confirmed with the customer — automatically by AI over WhatsApp or by phone, depending on order value. Unconfirmed orders are held rather than shipped. It is unglamorous and it is usually the single highest-return change we make.

Can you migrate our existing store?

Yes, including products, customers, order history and URL redirects so you do not lose search rankings. We run the old and new store in parallel until the new one is proven.
